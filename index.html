Hello there! Let me introduce myself...

Hi! I'm David, a Computer Engineer, Senior Game Developer, and multidisciplinary creator with more than 7 years of experience developing large-scale mobile games and interactive applications using Unity, C#, and Java.

Throughout my career, I’ve worked on live products with millions of users, focusing on gameplay systems, software architecture, optimization, tooling, and scalable development pipelines. I enjoy solving complex technical challenges while building experiences that feel polished, responsive, and meaningful for players.

Video games have been part of my life since childhood. I’ve always been fascinated not only by their technical side, but also by their artistic, emotional, and cultural impact. That passion naturally led me to explore concept art, visual design, and game aesthetics alongside programming, shaping a hybrid technical-artistic profile that allows me to approach development from multiple perspectives.

I strongly value collaboration, creativity, and positive team environments. I believe the best projects are built by people who enjoy working together, sharing knowledge, and constantly learning from one another. My self-driven mindset and adaptability have helped me grow across different disciplines, from gameplay programming and system design to art implementation and creative problem-solving.

I’m always excited by new challenges, ambitious ideas, and opportunities to create memorable interactive experiences.
